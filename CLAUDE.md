# Working on this repository

Read `FARUQI.md` first. It sets out the Faruqian core, al-tawḥīd as Ismāʿīl Rājī al-Fārūqī expounded it in *Al Tawhid: Its Implications for Thought and Life*, which runs through the themes and content of this site and its eight sister sites. Do not deviate from it. `SSOT.md` holds the fixed facts of this site.
