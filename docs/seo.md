# Keyword plan

Source: Semrush exports supplied on 25 September 2026: US "laylat al-qadr" all keywords (10,003 rows, 140,690 monthly searches), Malaysia "lailatulqadar" broad match (75 rows, 600), Indonesia "lailatulqadar" broad match (18 rows, 230). The full keyword-by-keyword assignment is in `lailatulqadar-keyword-map.xlsx`, delivered with 1.1.0.

## Targeted share

About 39 percent of the US volume (54,830 searches) is on topic and assigned to a page. The remaining 61 percent is not targeted, for these reasons:

| Reason | US volume |
|---|---|
| Other occasions (Eid al-Adha, Arafah, Dhul Hijjah, Hajj, qurbani, sadaqah, kaffarah) | 84,510 |
| Qadar as divine decree, a separate doctrine | 1,050 |
| Non-English queries (served by the Malay pages) | 100 |
| Media and quotes (images, drama, quotes) | 200 |

Targeting the off-topic clusters would dilute the site's topical focus on one night.

## Rules

- Title tags: 60 characters at most (agency template). Meta descriptions: 130 characters at most, ending with a call to action (house rule, stricter than the template's 140).
- Progress, content briefs and deadlines are tracked in `lailatulqadar.guide - SEO Work Progress (2026).xlsx`.
- One H1 per page, carrying the page's primary keyword in the house spelling.
- Dated pages (When Is, Last Ten Nights) take their year from `[lq_ramadan]`, so titles, descriptions and body roll over automatically the day after Eid.
- Recheck volumes with a fresh export in December 2026, before the season.
